## Strategic read
- Equal-weight 4x5 rubric: a polished, working, clearly demoed project beats an ambitious half-built one (Execution) - and AI must be visibly central, not a chat wrapper.
- Small field (56) and one gift card: execution and a crisp 2-3 min video decide it; spend real time on the video.
- Beginner-friendly, so judges likely favor clarity and usefulness over deep technical novelty; Creativity comes from a fresh angle on a familiar problem.
- The theme "Remote Control AI / take control of what AI can do" hints at human-in-the-loop control of AI agents (approval, steering, guardrails, remote operation) - fitting is a cheap Creativity win but is not required.
- Avoid: scope creep, missing README/run instructions, unlisted/broken video link, anything that needs paid keys judges can't run (provide a demo mode).
